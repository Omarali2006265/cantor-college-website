Information For Students

At Cantor College, we are committed to providing students with world-class facilities and support to help you succeed in your studies and personal development.

Our Facilities

1. Advanced Computing Labs

Equipped with the latest hardware and software for programming, cybersecurity, data science, and more. Labs are accessible 24/7.

2. Design Studios

Creative spaces for graphic design, digital media, and product design, with professional software, high-end tablets, large-format printers, and collaborative areas.

3. Innovation and Makerspace

Tools for prototyping and invention, including 3D printers, laser cutters, and CNC machines. Ideal for turning ideas into real projects.

4. Technology Sandbox

An experimental space for VR, AR, and AI technologies, allowing students to explore emerging tech hands-on.

5. Collaborative Learning Spaces

Spaces designed for teamwork and problem-solving, equipped with smartboards, video conferencing tools, and flexible seating arrangements.

6. Library and Resource Centre

A comprehensive library with books, journals, digital resources, computer stations, quiet study areas, and online database access.

7. Student Hub

The social heart of campus, featuring a café, lounges, and recreational facilities for relaxation, socializing, and student-led activities.

8. Career and Development Centre

Career counseling, resume workshops, job placement services, and employer networking events to prepare you for the professional world.

9. Fitness and Wellness Centre

Gym, exercise studios, wellness services including yoga, meditation, and counseling to support your physical and mental well-being.

10. On-Campus Housing

Modern, comfortable residence halls with high-speed internet, study lounges, and common areas to support a vibrant student community.

Experience Cantor College

Our facilities are designed to enhance learning, foster creativity, and support personal growth. Explore, engage, and make the most of everything Cantor College offers.
