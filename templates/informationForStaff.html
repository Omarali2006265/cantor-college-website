Information For Staff

Cantor College provides a range of services and resources to support our staff in their roles. Below is an overview of key areas and how to access them.

News

Cantor College recently hosted the Software Engineering Student of the Year Awards. Shortlisted candidates from around the country attended this one-day event.

Academic Registry

Responsible for academic regulations, assessments, awards, student records, and course validation. Contact the Academic Registry for queries in these areas.

Catering Services

Contact Catering Services for on-site or off-site catering requests for staff and events.

Financial Services

The financial team, based on the 2nd Floor, handles student finance, College budgeting, and related financial matters.

Information Systems Services (ISS)

The ISS team delivers computing facilities including hardware, software, and the staff helpdesk. Contact them for IT support or system issues.

Marketing Services

The Marketing Team supports event promotion, press releases, and public relations. Contact them for help with communications or marketing initiatives.

Personnel Services

All staff pay, conditions, and HR enquiries should be directed to the Personnel Services team on the 3rd floor.

Facilities

The Facilities team is responsible for the general care and maintenance of the College. All enquiries should be made via the main helpdesk.
